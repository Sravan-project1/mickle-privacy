This Privacy Policy explains how the Mickle mobile application ("Mickle," "the App," "we," "our," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use it.
Mickle is operated by Sravan Kumar Sattar, a sole proprietor ("the Data Fiduciary" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). Mickle is a free, ad-supported mobile application that displays live and historical gold and silver prices for India and global markets, along with related features such as price alerts, AI-generated market insights, and a premium-features waitlist.
By installing or using Mickle, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
1. Summary in plain English

Mickle does not require an account. We do not know who you are.
Mickle does not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or any government identification.
Mickle does not sell your data to anyone, ever.
Mickle does not ask for any payment information.
Mickle uses Google AdMob to show ads, which uses your device's advertising identifier.
Mickle uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send price-alert push notifications you opt into.
Mickle stores anonymous device-generated tokens in our database to power features like alerts and the waitlist.
Mickle's data may be processed outside India by our service providers (such as Google, Anthropic, Supabase).

If you are comfortable with the above, you can stop reading. The rest of this document explains the details.
2. Information we collect
2.1 Information you provide directly
You can use most of Mickle without providing any information. The only data you actively provide is:

Price alert preferences — when you create an alert, you choose a metal (gold or silver), a condition (above/below), and a target price. We store this along with an anonymous device token. We do not know who you are.
Waitlist signal — if you tap "Join Waitlist" on the Premium screen, we record an anonymous device token to count interest. No email, name, or contact information is collected.

2.2 Information collected automatically
When you use the App, the following may be collected automatically by us or our service providers:

Anonymous device tokens — randomly generated identifiers used to associate alerts and waitlist entries with a specific app install. These are not linked to your real identity.
Push notification token — issued by Firebase Cloud Messaging (operated by Google) when you opt into price alerts. Used solely to deliver those notifications.
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) — used by Google AdMob to show ads. You can reset or limit this at any time (see Section 5).
Standard mobile diagnostics — operating system version, app version, crash logs, device model, and similar non-identifying technical data, collected by Google Play Services.
App interaction logs — anonymous server logs of which prices/cities/charts you view, used to monitor App performance. These logs do not identify you.

2.3 Information we do not collect
For clarity, Mickle does not collect:

Your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or date of birth
Government identifiers (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport, etc.)
Bank account, credit card, UPI, or any payment information
Photographs, contacts, calendar, location, microphone, camera, or storage access
Biometric or health data
Browsing history outside the App

3. How we use your information
We use the limited data we collect only to:

Deliver Mickle's core features (live prices, historical charts, alerts, AI insights, waitlist)
Send push notifications you have explicitly opted into
Display ads to keep the App free for users
Diagnose crashes and improve App performance and reliability
Anonymously measure interest in the upcoming Premium tier

We do not use your data for:

Identity verification, profiling, credit scoring, or lending decisions
Automated decisions that affect you legally or significantly
Resale to data brokers, marketers, or any third party
Building a profile linked to you as an individual

Legal basis for processing
We process the limited data described above on the legal basis of your consent under the DPDP Act, 2023, given when you install and use the App and (where applicable) when you opt into specific features such as push notifications.

5. Advertising
Mickle is supported by ads served via Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) to show ads, including ads that may be more relevant to you based on your interests inferred by Google.
Google AdMob and its ad-network partners may use cookies, advertising identifiers, and similar technologies. Information about Google's ad practices is available at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
You can control or limit ad personalisation at any time:

Open your device's Settings
Go to Google → Ads
Select "Reset advertising ID" to get a new anonymous identifier
Select "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" to receive non-personalised ads only

You will continue to see ads (since they keep Mickle free), but they will not be personalised based on your inferred interests.
For more detail, see Google's ad personalisation help: https://support.google.com/ads/answer/2662922
6. AI-generated content
Mickle includes an "AI Insights" feature that generates short market commentary on gold and silver prices. This commentary is produced by an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) using publicly available market data such as recent prices and historical trends. We do not send any user-specific data to the AI service.
The AI-generated content is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not financial advice, investment advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset. You should consult a qualified financial advisor before making investment decisions.
7. Push notifications
If you opt into price alerts, Mickle will send push notifications to your device when a price condition you configured is met. You can disable push notifications at any time:

Open your device's Settings
Find Apps → Mickle → Notifications
Turn off notifications

You can also delete individual alerts inside the App's Alerts tab.
8. Children's privacy
Mickle is not intended for children. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, "child" means an individual under 18 years of age in India. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. Children should not use Mickle without verifiable parental consent.
If you believe a child has used Mickle in a way that resulted in any data collection, please contact us using the details in Section 12 and we will take appropriate steps, including deleting any associated data.
9. Data retention and deletion

Anonymous device tokens, alerts, and waitlist entries are retained for as long as the install is active and for a reasonable period afterwards (typically up to 12 months) for operational continuity.
Price-history data in our database is anonymous market data and contains no user information.
Diagnostic logs are retained for up to 90 days and then deleted or anonymised.

You can stop all further data collection at any time by uninstalling Mickle from your device.
If you would like us to delete the anonymous tokens associated with your install (for example, before uninstalling, you want a fresh start), email us at the address in Section 12. Because we do not know who you are, we may need additional information from you (such as the exact device token shown in the App's "About" or "Settings" area) to honour the request.
10. Your rights under India's DPDP Act, 2023
If you are a resident of India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") gives you the following rights regarding any personal data we hold about you:

Right to access information about your personal data we process
Right to correction of inaccurate personal data, and right to erasure of personal data no longer needed
Right of grievance redressal — to raise complaints to us about how we handle your data
Right to nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of incapacity or death

Because Mickle does not collect personal information that identifies you (no name, email, phone number, or government ID), most of these rights have limited practical application — there is no personal data tied to your identity for us to access, correct, or erase. However, you may still contact us using the details in Section 12 to exercise any applicable right, and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 30 days).

11. Security
We protect the limited data we hold using industry-standard practices, including:

HTTPS encryption for all network traffic between the App and our servers
Secure API tokens for backend access
Encrypted storage at rest by our database provider (Supabase)
Least-privilege access controls on all systems
No storage of credentials or sensitive identifiers on user devices

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps. In the unlikely event of a personal data breach affecting users, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users in accordance with the DPDP Act.
